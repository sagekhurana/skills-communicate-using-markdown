# Paleontology
## What is paleontology?
Paleontology is the study if ancient life and environments on Earth and how they have changed through geologic time.
## Who studies paleontology?
A paleontologist studies paleontology through fossils, which are the physical or trace remains of organisms from the past. These include fossilized bones, fossilized shells, permineralized wood, lithified footprints, etc.
